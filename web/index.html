How ParcelWatch works

  1. Download. USDA NAIP aerial imagery for 2018 and 2022 (0.6 m, 4 bands), Wake County parcels and building permits, Overture building footprints.
  2. Prepare. Both years go on one pixel grid in UTM 17N. The offset between years is measured at road intersections and sets the match tolerance.
  3. Detect. The same building model runs on both years with the same settings.
  4. Compare. Buildings are matched as objects, never by pixel differencing. Each one is new, expanded (+20 m² and 1.2×), unchanged or demolished.
  5. Check permits. Each changed parcel is checked for a building permit issued from Oct 2017 to Oct 2022, by location or parcel number.
  6. Validate and price. 200 parcels are hand-checked. Missed tax uses the median value per sq ft of recent homes, 2026 tax rates, and is multiplied by measured precision.

Limitations

  • Tree cover and shadows hide some roofs and fool the model.
  • NAIP is accurate to about ±6 m, so small additions can be missed.
  • Only roofed buildings count. Pools, decks and patios aren't detected.
  • Fire district tax is left out, so dollar figures are conservative.
